Formula

The structure to remember

[Who] + [reporting verb in the past] + that + [what they said, in the past]

Direct speech

“AI will replace many routine jobs.”

Reported speech

Lee said that AI would replace many routine jobs.

Grammar shifts

Three changes to check fast

⏪

Tense shift

is/are → was/were, do/does → did, have/has → had, will → would, can → could, may → might.

🧭

Pronoun shift

Change the speaker’s point of view: I → he/she/they, we → they, my → his/her/their, our → their.

🧩

Modal check

Some modals change, but would, could, might, and should usually stay the same in reported speech.

Example

“We can contribute.” → Amara said that they could contribute.

Still true now?

Keeping the present can happen, but shifting back is always a safe choice.

Word choice

Pick the right reporting verb

Neutral

said, told us/me

Use these when you simply want to report information.

Strong opinion

argued, claimed

Choose them when the speaker defended a position or made an assertion.

Ideas and evidence

suggested, found, reported

These fit recommendations, research results, or formal sources.

Concern or honesty

warned, admitted

Use them for danger, concern, or reluctant acknowledgment.

Practice language

Discussion Expressions

Stating a position

  • I believe that…
  • I would argue that…
  • From where I stand…
  • The reality is…

Conceding

  • I recognize that…
  • I’m not saying… What I am saying is…
  • I understand what you’re saying…
  • That may be true, but…

Responding

  • That’s a fair point…
  • You raise an important point…
  • I hadn’t thought of it that way…
  • I agree, and I’d add that…
  • I get your point, but in my experience…
  • I’d push back on that a little…

Reaching consensus

  • So we seem to agree that…
  • Perhaps the middle ground is…
  • Can we say that…
  • We can both agree that…

Model answer

From discussion to reported speech

Live discussion

A student says, “I believe that AI can improve customer support.” Another student replies, “That’s a fair point, but companies should think about bias.”

Reported version

The first student said that they believed AI could improve customer support. The second student replied that it was a fair point, but companies should think about bias.

Quick self-check

Did you shift the tense, change the pronouns, choose the right reporting verb, use modal verbs correctly, and include that?
